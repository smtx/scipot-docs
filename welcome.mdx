## What is SciPot?

SciPot is a **trust layer for agent memory**. You feed it documents, conversations, and structured data. It transforms them into **POTs** — Knowledge Pots — where every fact carries a numeric POT Score (Proof of Truth), every score traces to a source, and every contradiction is detected and flagged.

Your AI agents query SciPot and receive answers with **receipts**: cited facts, certainty levels, and an epistemic briefing of what the answer is confident about versus where coverage is thin.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.scipot.ai/pots/{pot_id}/synthesize" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $SCIPOT_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "query": "What did we decide about pricing for enterprise?",
    "include_epistemic_briefing": true
  }'
```

Response includes the synthesized answer, the facts used, their POT Scores, source provenance, and any contradictions detected during synthesis.
